Assurance & Verification

Comprehensive
Audit Support

Ensure absolute transparency and statutory compliance. Our expert audit assistance streamlines your verification processes, mitigates regulatory risks, and prepares your financial data for rigorous internal and external evaluations.

Internal Audit Support

Proactive evaluation of your company’s internal controls, risk management frameworks, and governance processes. We help identify operational inefficiencies, prevent capital leakage, and ensure your standard operating procedures are strictly followed.

Statutory Audit Support

End-to-end assistance in preparing your financial statements in accordance with the Companies Act and relevant accounting standards. We act as a bridge between your management team and external auditors to ensure a smooth, discrepancy-free audit cycle.

Tax Audit Support

Meticulous reconciliation of your financial data to align with Income Tax Act requirements. We assist in generating accurate Form 3CD schedules, identifying inadmissible expenses, and minimizing the risk of tax penalties or departmental scrutiny.

Stock Audit Assistance

Comprehensive physical verification of inventory assets across warehouses. We help reconcile physical stock against book records, identify shrinkage or obsolete goods, and ensure accurate valuation for financing and reporting purposes.
