Our CSR / Grant Services

We provide a holistic, end-to-end framework to secure and manage crucial institutional funding.

01

Project Identification

Strategic identification of suitable funding opportunities accurately mapped to the project's sector, location, and key beneficiaries.

02

Project Concept Note

Development of a highly professional concept note highlighting the exact project objectives, targeted beneficiaries, activities, and outcomes.

03

Detailed Project Report (DPR)

Structuring full DPRs including need assessments, robust implementation strategies, budgets, timelines, M&E frameworks, and expected impact metrics.

04

CSR Proposal Development

Crafting sophisticated, corporate-ready CSR proposals perfectly aligned with applicable legal frameworks and institutional funding priorities.

05

Documentation Support

Organizing 12A/80G, CSR Registration (CRN), PAN, audited financial statements, annual reports, governing body details, and previous experience logs.

06

Funding Outreach

Support in intelligently identifying and approaching suitable CSR foundations, major corporate donors, and strategic government departments.

07

Monitoring & Reporting

Post-funding support managing project monitoring, strict impact reporting, accurate utilization documentation, and transparent donor communications.
