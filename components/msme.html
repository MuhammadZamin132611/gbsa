Government Subsidies & Support

MSME
Schemes

Government initiatives designed to strengthen Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. Unlock powerful financial backing, technology upgrades, capacity building, and global market access to scale your business.

Udyam Registration

Streamlined online registration for MSMEs to gain official recognition, unlocking access to vital government subsidies, lower bank interest rates, and legal protections.

PMEGP

Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme. Secure heavy financial assistance and capital subsidies for setting up new micro-enterprises and generating employment.

CGTMSE

Credit Guarantee Fund Trust. Secure massive, collateral-free credit lines and term loans from banks, backed directly by the government to fuel your business expansion.

MSME Champions

A unified technology-driven portal providing single-window grievance redressal, strategic guidance, and holistic support to help MSMEs overcome operational hurdles.

Procurement & Marketing

Access lucrative new markets. We guide you through participating in major trade fairs and securing high-value government tenders through mandatory MSME quotas.

International Cooperation

Facilitating global exposure, technology infusion, and export promotion through heavily subsidized participation in international exhibitions and global trade delegations.

MSE Cluster Development

Upgrading infrastructure and establishing Common Facility Centres (CFCs) for MSME clusters to drastically enhance regional productivity and global competitiveness.

Skill Development

Accessing government-backed Entrepreneurship and Skill Development Programmes (ESDP) designed to upgrade your workforce's technical acumen and industry readiness.

RAMP Initiative

Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance. A World Bank-assisted scheme enhancing center-state convergence, market access, and overall institutional resilience.

SFURTI

Scheme of Fund for Regeneration of Traditional Industries. Organizing traditional artisans into specialized clusters to provide equipment, training, and long-term sustainability.

National SC-ST Hub

Providing professional support, capacity building, and financial aid to SC/ST entrepreneurs to dramatically increase their participation in public procurement ecosystems.
