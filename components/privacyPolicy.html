Data Protection & Transparency

Privacy
Policy

Global Business & Startup Association (GBSA) deeply respects the privacy of its members, visitors, partners, and stakeholders. We are committed to safeguarding your information with the highest standards of integrity.

Information We Collect

To provide you with a tailored and seamless experience, information collected through our website and platforms may include:

Name

Full legal or preferred name for personalized communication.

Email Address

Primary contact email for updates and digital correspondence.

Mobile Number

Direct contact number for urgent alerts and WhatsApp integration.

Organization Details

Company name, sector, and core operational data.

Professional Info

Designation, expertise, and business background.

Membership Info

Status, tier level, and associated networking preferences.

Enquiry Details

Any specific messages, project proposals, or consulting requests you submit through our contact portals.

Use of Information

The information we collect is strictly utilized for the following functional, administrative, and developmental purposes:

Membership Processing

To seamlessly onboard, verify, and manage your active GBSA membership profile.

Responding to Enquiries

To provide prompt, accurate answers to your questions, consultancy requests, or support needs.

Event Registration

To facilitate your ticketing, attendance, and badging for summits, workshops, and networking meets.

Business Communication

To connect you with potential partners, investors, and relevant ecosystem stakeholders.

Providing Services

To deliver the specific government scheme consultancy, training, or strategic advisory you requested.

Improving Website Experience

To analyze digital traffic trends and enhance our platform's UI/UX and operational functionality.

Sending Relevant Updates

To keep you informed about new government schemes, upcoming events, newsletters, and critical industry developments.

Our Security Commitment

GBSA will take reasonable and necessary technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information and will not knowingly misuse, sell, or unlawfully distribute any personal data collected.

Note: This is a draft representation of the privacy framework. The final Privacy Policy should be thoroughly reviewed and approved by your legal advisor before official publishing to ensure full compliance with applicable data protection laws.
