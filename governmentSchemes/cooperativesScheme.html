Cooperative Schemes
& Funding Support

Empowering Cooperatives Through Government Schemes, Finance & Business Development. Cooperatives play an important role in strengthening rural economies, creating employment, improving market access, and promoting inclusive economic growth.

Our Advisory & Project Support Services help cooperative societies, PACS, dairy/fisheries cooperatives, and FPOs understand relevant schemes and prepare professionally structured projects for financial assistance.

Key Cooperative Schemes & Initiatives

1. PACS Computerization

Support for modernization and digital transformation of PACS, including technology infrastructure, accounting systems, and improved service delivery.

Suitable for: PACS & Cooperative Credit Institutions

2. Promotion of FPOs

Cooperative societies can participate in developing FPOs to improve aggregation, collective marketing, value addition, branding, and export opportunities.

3. Multipurpose PACS

Developing PACS as rural business entities for agriculture inputs, procurement, food processing, and dairy in Panchayats currently lacking them.

4. Grain Storage Plan

Utilizing the cooperative sector to strengthen decentralized storage, including warehouse development, primary processing, and market linkage.

5. PACS as CSCs

Eligible PACS can provide citizen-oriented digital and government services by functioning as Common Service Centres, diversifying revenue sources.

6. Jan Aushadhi Kendras

Eligible PACS can establish Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Kendras to create additional service and business opportunities at the grassroots level.

7. LPG Distributorship

PACS have been made eligible for participation in LPG distributorship opportunities, subject to applicable rules, criteria, and selection procedures.

8. Petrol & Diesel Dealership

Eligible PACS can receive priority in petrol and diesel dealership opportunities under applicable government allocation guidelines.

NCDC Financial Assistance

The National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC) plays an important role in supporting cooperative development through robust financial assistance and development programmes.

Government programme information also identifies support for marketing, processing, storage, consumer programmes, PACS computerization, and other vital cooperative development activities.
