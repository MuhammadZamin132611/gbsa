Who Needs FSSAI?

By law, any entity involved in the manufacturing, processing, storage, distribution, or sale of food products must be FSSAI compliant.

Food Manufacturers

Large-scale factories and production plants manufacturing raw or finished food products.

Food Processors

Entities involved in agro-processing, value addition, and preservation of raw agricultural produce.

Restaurants

Fine-dining establishments, QSRs, cafes, and local eateries serving prepared food to customers.

Cloud Kitchens

Delivery-only ghost kitchens operating online food delivery models without dine-in facilities.

Caterers

Event caterers, banquet food providers, and institutional food service operators.

Food Traders

Wholesalers, distributors, suppliers, and retail shop owners handling food commodities.

Importers

Businesses importing foreign food products, ingredients, or additives into the Indian market.

Exporters

Companies exporting domestic agricultural produce or processed foods to international buyers.

Packaged Food

FMCG companies, snack manufacturers, and beverage brands dealing in pre-packaged items.

Food Startups

Innovative D2C food brands, health-food ventures, and dynamic food-tech platforms.
