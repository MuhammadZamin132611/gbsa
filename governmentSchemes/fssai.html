FSSAI Schemes by Government

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) is responsible for ensuring the safety and quality of food products across the country. FSSAI schemes promote safe, hygienic, and nutritious food while helping food businesses comply with national regulations.

Professional support for food business compliance.

Safe disposal and reuse of used cooking oil.

Trans Fat Free India

Eliminating harmful trans fats from food products.
