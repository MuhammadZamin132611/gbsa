About Global Business & Startup Association

Global Business & Startup Association Venture Private Limited is a Delhi-based technology and skill-development company established to bridge the widening gap between traditional education and the AI-driven global economy. The company serves as the Indian operations arm of is committed to building an AI-ready workforce through global education, industry certifications, practical training and placement support.

Vision

To become India's most trusted Artificial Intelligence Talent Hub producing globally employable AI professionals through innovation, ethical technology education and industry-integrated learning.

Mission

Train and place more than 100,000 youth, graduates and professionals in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Analytics, Automation and Emerging Technologies by 2030.
