II. Debt Funding & Hybrid Instruments

Protect equity structures using specialized non-dilutive loan portfolios, infrastructure financing, and cross-border hybrid debt models.

⚖️

Mezzanine Finance

Integrate flexible hybrid capital vehicles that occupy the space between senior debt systems and pure enterprise equity. We structure custom subordinated loan models featuring convertible embedded warrants to safely fund massive organic expansions or buyout targets.

🌉

Bridge Finance

Resolve short-term runway leaks between major institutional investment actions. Our rapid advisory pipelines configure reliable short-term debt assets to sustain operational momentum safely while finalizing primary multi-year venture rounds.

🏗️

Project Finance

Fund major capital assets, manufacturing lines, and heavy setups through non-recourse or limited-recourse models. We build independent special purpose vehicles (SPVs) designed to isolate project balance sheets safely from parent corporate assets.

Structured Finance

Mitigate complex financial risks using bespoke capital market assets. We structure specialized instruments—including asset-backed securities (ABS), customized collateral bundles, and synthetic risk allocations—tailored explicitly for advanced institutional balancing actions.
