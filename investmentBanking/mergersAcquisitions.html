II. Deal Mechanics & Execution

Mitigate regulatory vulnerabilities, optimize structural variables, and protect transaction economics.

📊

Business Valuation

Establish a robust and defensible enterprise asset calculation. We utilize cross-checked valuation metrics—including discounted cash flow (DCF) pipelines, comparable public market trading multiples, and preceding sector transaction records to ensure precision pricing.

🔍

Due Diligence

Isolate hidden system risks and identify exposure points before committing capital assets. Our comprehensive audit workflows evaluate tax liabilities, review material commercial contracts, check employment continuity agreements, and run intellectual property verification.

⚖️

Deal Structuring

Balance risk and reward using customized financial engineering models. We design transaction architectures balancing asset vs. stock transitions, constructing appropriate earn-out structures, structuring rollover equity conditions, and optimizing the post-close tax footprint.

🛡️

Negotiation Support

Maintain transactional leverage through complex boardroom dialogue cycles. Our strategic advisory desks manage position papers, establish clear valuation boundaries, and resolve communication blockades while moving toward definitive purchase agreements.

📈

Mitigating Transaction Slip

A deal's economic success relies on protecting baseline value through integration. By synchronizing transaction conditions with post-merger governance protocols, we protect your organization against value drop-off.
